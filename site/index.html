Sample

Keep a list of the things that matter to you.

Getting started

Tap + to add an item. Tap an item to see it, or touch and hold it to delete it.

Reminders

Turn on a daily reminder in Settings and pick its time. If reminders don't arrive, check that notifications are on for Sample in the iPhone Settings app.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone, and your iCloud or computer backup includes it. See the privacy policy.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub.
