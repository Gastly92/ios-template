Privacy policy

Sample

What the app collects

Nothing. Sample has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. It doesn't track you or share anything with anyone.

Where your data lives

Your items and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Your iCloud or computer backup includes them, like other apps' data, under your Apple account's own privacy terms.

Reminders

Daily reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in the iPhone Settings app, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage numbers. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
